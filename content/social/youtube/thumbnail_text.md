## Thumbnail Design Notes

- Dark background (#0a0a0f) matching the dashboard
- Cyan accent (#06b6d4) for numbers/highlights
- White for main text
- Font: Inter or Geist (bold weight for main text, regular for subtext)
- Include a small screenshot of the attribution report on the right side
- Keep main text under 5 words for mobile readability
- Size: 1280×720px, export as JPG
